# How we use your data — Digiability Community

**Version:** 2026-10-02

This notice is shown before you agree to let us process your personal data, as required by
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act §5 and §6). The full detail is in
our [Privacy Policy](./01-privacy-policy.md).

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] is responsible for your data.

## What we collect, and why

- **Name, email address and password** (the password is stored only as a secure hash) — to
  create your account and let you sign in.
- **Phone number** — so you can be reached about your account.
- **Date of birth** — to confirm you are 18 or older. Digiability is an adult platform.
- **The roles you select** (for example caregiver or mentor) — to show you the right features
  and communities.
- **Profile details** — city, gender, disability type and history, support needs, professional
  credentials — to connect you with relevant people, groups and services. **All optional.**
  Disability information is shared only if you choose to add it.
- **Messages, group chats, forum posts and votes** — to deliver them to the people you are
  talking to.
- **Your device's push token and platform** — to send you notifications. You can turn these
  off.
- **Last-seen time and login sessions** — to keep you signed in securely and show when you
  were last active.
- **Reports, moderation decisions and grievances** — to keep the community safe and meet our
  legal duties.
- **Your consent records, with a shortened IP address** — to prove what you agreed to, and
  when.

We do not show advertising and we do not sell your data.

## Who else handles it

Our hosting provider, our push-notification providers (Expo, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging,
Apple), our email provider, and content-moderation services process data on our behalf. The
full list, and where each one stores data, is in Section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. After deletion, a minimal registration record is kept for
180 days because Indian intermediary rules require it; then it is erased. Other periods are in
Section 8 of the Privacy Policy.

## Your choices

- **See and download your data:** Profile → My data & privacy → View my data
- **Correct it:** Profile → My data & privacy → Correct my details
- **Delete your account and data:** Profile → My data & privacy → Delete my account
- **Withdraw consent:** we cannot run your account without this consent, so withdrawing it
  means deleting your account. Optional consents (notifications, marketing) can be switched off
  at any time.
- **Complain:** contact our Grievance Officer — details in Section 11 of the Privacy Policy —
  or the Data Protection Board of India.
