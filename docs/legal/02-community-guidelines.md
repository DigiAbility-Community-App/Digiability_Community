## Our purpose

Digiability Community exists so that people with disabilities and the people who support them can connect, share, and get help without fear. Accessibility isn't a feature here — it's the point.

That means this community only works if everyone can participate safely. These guidelines apply to everything on Digiability: posts, comments, profiles, images, Care Circles, and direct messages.

**By using Digiability, you agree to follow these guidelines.** Breaking them can cost you your content, your account, or in serious cases, a report to law enforcement.

---

## What we're for

- **Assume good faith.** People here are often navigating hard things. Ask before you judge.
- **Respect how people describe themselves.** Identity-first or person-first language, disclosed or private diagnoses — that's each person's call, not yours.
- **Share experience, not prescriptions.** "Here's what worked for me" is welcome. "You should stop your treatment" is not.
- **Protect each other's privacy.** What's shared in a Care Circle stays there.
- **Make your contributions accessible.** Add alt text to images, caption video where you can, write in plain language, and don't rely on colour or emoji alone to carry meaning. See "Accessibility etiquette" below.

## What's not allowed

These are the behaviours and content we consider **objectionable**. They are prohibited anywhere on Digiability.

### 1. Harassment, bullying, and threats
No targeting, demeaning, intimidating, or humiliating people. This includes coordinated pile-ons, repeated unwanted contact after someone has blocked or asked you to stop, threats of violence, and encouraging others to harass someone.

### 2. Hate speech and discrimination
No attacks, slurs, dehumanisation, or calls for exclusion based on disability, race, ethnicity, caste, religion, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or any other protected characteristic. **Ableism — including inspiration porn, mocking disability, and speaking over disabled people about their own lives — is hate speech here.**

### 3. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)
Absolutely prohibited, without exception. Any content that sexualises a minor, or any attempt to groom, solicit, or exploit a minor, results in immediate removal, a permanent ban, and a report to the appropriate authorities.

Digiability is an 18+ platform. We report child sexual abuse material to Indian law enforcement through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and to the local police, as required under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012. Where a report to an international clearing house is also appropriate, we make one.

See our [Child Safety Standards](./04-child-safety-standards.md).

### 4. Sexual content and nudity
No sexually explicit content, nudity, or sexual solicitation. This is a support community, not an adult platform.

### 5. Violence and dangerous content
No graphic violence, gore, glorification of violence, or content promoting dangerous acts.

### 6. Self-harm and suicide
No content that encourages, instructs, or glorifies self-harm, suicide, or disordered eating. **You are welcome to talk about your own struggles** — that's part of why this community exists. The line is between sharing pain and promoting harm.

If you are in crisis, please reach out. In India you can call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** (free, 24×7, multiple languages), **KIRAN on 1800-599-0019**, or **Vandrevala Foundation on 9999 666 555**. In an emergency, call **112**. A fuller list is at [digiability.org/safety](https://digiability.org/safety) and in the app under Settings → Safety resources.

### 7. Harmful health misinformation
This one matters especially here. Do not present yourself as a medical professional if you aren't. Do not promote unproven cures, discourage people from seeking medical care, or push treatments that could hurt someone. Peer experience is valuable; medical advice from strangers is dangerous.

### 8. Illegal and regulated activity
No content facilitating illegal acts, sale of controlled substances or weapons, fraud, or trafficking.

### 9. Spam, scams, and manipulation
No bulk or repetitive posting, engagement farming, phishing, fake giveaways, pyramid schemes, or fundraising fraud. **Be aware: disability communities are actively targeted by scammers.** We treat financial exploitation of members with particular seriousness.

### 10. Impersonation and fake accounts
Don't pretend to be someone else — another person, a caregiver, a medical professional, an organisation, or Digiability staff. Don't run multiple accounts to evade a ban or manufacture consensus.

### 11. Privacy violations
No posting someone else's personal information without their clear consent — address, phone number, medical details, photos, workplace, or identity. Never share another member's disclosures outside the space where they shared them. **Doxxing results in an immediate ban.**

### 12. Intellectual property
Don't post content you don't have the right to post.

### 13. Circumventing enforcement
Don't evade blocks, bans, or content removals.

## Accessibility etiquette

Not rules with penalties — expectations that make this community what it says it is.

- Add **alt text** to images. If you can't, describe the image in your post.
- **Caption** video and audio where you can.
- Use **plain language**. Expand acronyms on first use.
- Don't use decorative or repeated emoji mid-sentence — screen readers read every one aloud.
- Write **descriptive link text**, not "click here."
- Use **CamelCase in hashtags** (#DisabilityPride, not #disabilitypride) so screen readers parse them.
- Don't rely on colour alone to convey meaning.

## Reporting and blocking

**Report** anything that breaks these guidelines. Every post, comment, profile, and message has a **Report** option. Choose the reason that fits — it helps us triage faster. We'll confirm we received your report and give you a reference number.

**Block** anyone you don't want contacting you. Blocking is available from any profile or conversation, and you can undo it at any time from Settings → Blocked users. You never need a reason, and we don't tell the person you blocked them.

Blocking stops direct messages **in both directions** — once you block someone, neither of you can send the other a direct message. Blocking does not remove either of you from groups or Care Circles you both belong to. If you share a group with someone you'd rather not, you can leave the group, or report them to us if they are breaking these guidelines.

For anything involving immediate danger to someone's life, contact emergency services first — in India, call **112** — and then report to us.

**To report child safety concerns:** childsafety@digiability.org, or use the "Child safety" reason in the in-app report menu. These go to a priority queue.

### If content shows you without your consent

If someone has posted content showing you nude, in a sexual act, or in a state of undress, or has impersonated you, you can report it directly to our Grievance Officer and we will remove it **within 24 hours** of receiving your complaint. You do not need to go through the standard queue. Contact details are at the end of this document.

## How we respond to reports

| Severity | We acknowledge | We act |
|---|---|---|
| CSAE, credible threats of violence, trafficking | Immediately | Immediately, plus a report to the authorities |
| Risk of self-harm | Within 4 hours | Same day, and we surface safety resources to the person at risk |
| Non-consensual intimate imagery, impersonation | Within 24 hours | Within 24 hours |
| Harassment, hate speech, sexual content | Within 24 hours | Within 72 hours |
| Spam and lower-severity reports | Within 72 hours | Within 7 days |

Court orders and lawful directions from a government authority are acted on within 36 hours.

## How we enforce

Reports are reviewed by our moderation team. We also use automated keyword screening to catch some categories of prohibited content before they are seen.

Depending on severity, history, and intent, we may:

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| **Content removed** | Content breaks a guideline |
| **Warning** | First-time, lower-severity |
| **Temporary suspension** | Serious or repeated violations |
| **Permanent ban** | Severe violations, or a pattern of them |
| **Immediate ban + report to authorities** | CSAE, credible threats of violence, trafficking |

Severe violations skip the ladder. There is no warning for CSAE.

## Appeals

If we action your content or account and you think we got it wrong, you can appeal:

- **How:** appeals@digiability.org, or the Appeal button on the notice we sent you.
- **When:** within **30 days** of the date we sent you the decision notice.
- **What happens:** we look at the decision again against the original evidence, with a reviewer who was not involved in it wherever our team size allows.
- **How long:** we aim to respond within **14 days**, and within **7 days** where your account has been suspended.

We may decline to review repeated appeals of the same decision.

**If you are still unsatisfied**, you may escalate to the Grievance Appellate Committee established under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, at [gac.gov.in](https://gac.gov.in), within 30 days of our decision on your appeal.
