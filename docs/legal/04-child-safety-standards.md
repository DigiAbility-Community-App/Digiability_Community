Digiability Community has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**.
This document sets out our standards, how to report a concern, and what we do when we receive
one. It is published in accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and Apple's
App Review Guidelines, and reflects our obligations under the Protection of Children from Sexual
Offences Act, 2012 (POCSO), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023.

## 1. Digiability is an 18+ service

We do not permit accounts for anyone under 18. We ask for date of birth at registration and
refuse registration to anyone below that age. The mechanism, its limits, and how we handle
under-18 accounts we discover are described in the
[Age Policy and Gate Spec](./06-age-policy-and-gate-spec.md).

A person under 18 may be included in a Care Circle under the account and supervision of their
parent or lawful guardian, who confirms that relationship and is responsible for that
participation. They do not hold their own account.

## 2. What is prohibited

The following are prohibited absolutely, with no exception and no warning:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form — image, video, audio, drawing, or text
- Content that sexualises a minor, including sexualised depictions of a fictional minor
- Grooming: building a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes
- Solicitation of a minor for sexual purposes, or of sexual content from a minor
- Sextortion, or threatening to distribute a minor's intimate images
- Trafficking, or facilitating the sexual exploitation of a minor
- Providing links, contacts, or instructions that direct anyone to any of the above
- Using the service to identify or contact a minor for any of the above

These prohibitions apply to every surface of the service — posts, comments, profiles, images,
direct messages, groups, and Care Circles — including private conversations.

## 4. How we respond

On receiving a credible CSAE report we:

1. **Act immediately** — we do not wait for the standard review queue. CSAE reports are
   prioritised above every other category.
2. **Remove the content** from public view.
3. **Preserve the evidence.** We retain the reported content, the account records, and the
   access logs required for a law-enforcement referral, even where the account is otherwise
   being deleted. Preservation overrides ordinary deletion and retention rules.
4. **Permanently ban the account**, and take reasonable steps to prevent the person
   re-registering.
5. **Report to the authorities.** We report child sexual abuse material to Indian law
   enforcement through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and to the
   local police, as required under POCSO. Where a report to an international clearing house is
   also appropriate, we make one.
6. **Support the person affected**, where they are a member — including surfacing the safety
   resources listed in the Community Guidelines.

There is no warning tier and no appeal on the merits for confirmed CSAE. A person who believes
they were banned in error may still write to the Grievance Officer.

## 5. What we do to prevent it

- **Age gate at registration** — see the [Age Policy](./06-age-policy-and-gate-spec.md)
- **Guardian consent capture** for any minor included in a Care Circle
- **Automated keyword screening** on submitted text, before it is published
- **Automated image screening** on uploaded images
- **A priority moderation queue** for child safety reports
- **Access logging** — every administrator view of message content is recorded, so the
  investigation of a report is itself auditable
